# Constitution
## Luna Waves
### Version 0.1 — 2026-06-29 — DRAFT

> This document is the center of this organization.
> Every AI, every collaborator, every Capability Kit must read it before acting.
> When in doubt, the answer is here.

## 1. Who we are

Luna Waves is an independent music project that creates intimate, reflective electronic music for authentic connection.

## 2. Why we exist

**Mission:**
We create moments of connection through music that helps people slow down, reflect, and feel less alone.

**The transformation we seek:**
We transform the experience of modern loneliness into moments of shared reflection. When someone listens to Luna Waves, we want them to feel seen, understood, and connected to something larger than themselves.

## 3. Who we speak to

Authentic music seekers aged 18-35 who value depth over breadth, who listen to full albums not just singles, who support artists directly, and who share music that means something to them. They find us through recommendations, Spotify algorithm, and word-of-mouth. They seek music that feels genuine, not manufactured.

## 4. How we speak

**Our voice:**
Authentic, intimate, thoughtful, warming, direct.

**What we do:**
- Use "we believe" instead of "we are the best"
- Thank people for being part of the journey
- Share the story behind the music (not just the music)
- Be vulnerable about process and creation

**What we never do:**
- Use clickbait or hype
- Buy fake followers or engagement
- Chase trends that contradict our voice
- Pretend to be something we're not

## 5. What we value

**Authenticity**
We create music we genuinely believe in, not what we think will go viral. Every track is personal.

**Connection**
Music is about connection — between artist and listener, between listeners, between moments. We prioritize connection over consumption.

**Simplicity**
We strip away the unnecessary. Our music, our visuals, our words — all serve the feeling, not complexity.

**Reflection**
We create music that encourages slowing down, looking inward, reflecting. We don't chase adrenaline; we cultivate presence.

**Directness**
We connect directly with our audience. No labels, no gatekeepers, no platform lock-in when possible.

## 6. Our capabilities

| Capability | Maturity | Description |
|------------|----------|-------------|
| Music | developing | Producing original electronic music |
| Website | nascent | Building and maintaining our website |
| Content | nascent | Creating written and visual content |
| Social | nascent | Managing social media presence |

## 7. Our languages

- Music: Electronic, ambient, downtempo — our primary medium
- Writing: Thoughtful, reflective, not promotional
- Visuals: Minimal, with warmth, not flashy
- Email: Direct, personal, intimate

## 8. What we will never do

- Use AI to fake authenticity (we'll use AI to help, not to pretend)
- Compromise artistic integrity for views or streams
- Chase trends that contradict our voice
- Treat our audience as numbers, not people
- Create content that doesn't align with our values

## 9. How we use technology and AI

We use technology as a tool, not a replacement for human judgment. AI helps us:
- Generate ideas and starting points
- Improve workflow efficiency
- Scale certain tasks

But every AI output passes through this Constitution before being accepted. The final decision — the artistic judgment, the strategic alignment, the authenticity — is always human.

## 10. How we evolve

This Constitution can be revised. But the core — mission, values, audience — is stable.
What changes are expressions, not purpose.

We'll review quarterly. Revisions happen when:
- We've learned something fundamental about who we are
- Our audience has shifted meaningfully
- Our values have deepened or clarified

We don't revise for:
- Tactical decisions
- Minor adjustments
- Trend following
